SpendFox Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · App: SpendFox (Android, package com.vpvision.spendfox)

In short
  • SpendFox works offline. The app has no internet permission.
  • We do not collect, upload, sell or share your personal or financial data.
  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone unless you choose to back it up or export it.

1. Who we are

SpendFox ("the app", "we") is a personal expense tracker and money manager published on Google Play by VPVision. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the information you enter only in its private storage on your device:

  • Transactions (amount, date, type, category, account, payee, notes, tags, spending rating)
  • Accounts, balances, budgets, categories, recurring payments and templates
  • Receipt photos you attach
  • App settings (currency, language, theme, reminders)

This data is never sent to us or to any third party by the app. We have no server and no access to it.

3. Permissions and optional features

Feature / permissionWhat it is used for
Notifications Daily reminder and bill/due-date reminders, and (if enabled) notices about automatically added transactions.
Run at start-up Re-schedules your reminders after the phone restarts.
Camera / photo picker (optional) Only when you attach a receipt. The photo is copied into the app's private storage. The system photo picker gives the app access only to the photo you pick.
Notification access – Auto-capture (optional, off by default) If you turn it on, SpendFox reads notifications only from the payment, banking and SMS apps you allow to detect transactions (for example a UPI payment or a bank SMS alert). The text is processed entirely on your device. Messages that are not transactions (such as personal chats or OTPs) are ignored. For detected transactions the app keeps only the extracted details (amount, payee, date, last digits of the account) and a one-way fingerprint (hash) used to avoid duplicates. The original message text is not stored and nothing is uploaded. SpendFox does not request SMS permission and cannot read your SMS inbox. You can turn this off at any time in the app or in Android settings.
Share to SpendFox (optional) Text you share into the app (for example a bank message) is parsed on your device to suggest a transaction.

4. Backups and exports (your choice)

  • Manual and automatic backups are written to a folder you choose (for example on your phone, or a folder synced by Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox). Backups can be protected with a password you set; the password is not sent anywhere. If you choose a cloud-synced folder, that provider handles the file under its own privacy policy.
  • Android device backup: if you enable automatic backup in the app, Android's built-in backup may copy the latest encrypted SpendFox backup file to your Google account so you can restore it on a new phone. This is managed by Google under Google's privacy policy; the file can only be opened with your backup password.
  • Exports (CSV, PDF, JSON) are created only when you ask, and saved where you choose.

5. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The optional PIN lock is stored as a salted hash, not as plain text. Backup files are encrypted (AES-256) when you set a password. Please keep your phone and backup password secure; we cannot recover a lost backup password.

6. Third parties

SpendFox contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and does not share data with third parties. Fonts are provided by Android/Google Play services on your device; this does not involve your personal or financial data.

7. Children

SpendFox is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

8. Your control and data deletion

  • You can edit or delete any record at any time.
  • In the app, Settings → Delete & reset erases all data.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored by it on your phone. Backup or export files you saved elsewhere remain under your control and can be deleted by you.

Because we do not collect your data, we have no copy of it to access, correct or delete on our side.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
